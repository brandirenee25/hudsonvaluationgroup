Privacy

Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Hudson Valuation Group collects through this website and how it is used.

Effective date: October 12, 2026

Hudson Valuation Group is a California residential real estate appraisal business operated by Brandi Hudson, California Certified Residential Appraiser #AR029078.

Information we collect

When you submit the appraisal request form on our contact page, we receive the information you enter:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Property address (optional)
  • Purpose of the appraisal
  • County
  • Any additional details you choose to provide

We also receive information you send us directly by email or phone.

How the contact form is processed

The contact form is processed by Formspree, a third-party form service. Information you submit is transmitted to Formspree and delivered to us by email. Formspree’s handling of that information is described in its own privacy policy.

Analytics and cookies

This website uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site, such as which pages are viewed. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies and collects information such as pages visited, approximate location, device and browser type. You can limit cookies through your browser settings.

Our scheduling pages embed Calendly, a third-party scheduling service. If you book a consultation, the information you provide is handled by Calendly under its own privacy policy.

Website hosting

This website is hosted by Vercel. As with most websites, the hosting provider may process technical information such as IP address, browser type, and request logs as needed to deliver and secure the site, under its own privacy policy.

California Do Not Track and Third-Party Tracking

Some web browsers offer a “Do Not Track” (DNT) setting. Because there is no common standard for responding to these signals, this website does not currently respond differently to DNT signals.

Third-party services used on this website, including analytics (Google Analytics), scheduling (Calendly), form processing (Formspree), and website hosting (Vercel), may collect information about your online activity over time and across websites in accordance with their own privacy policies. You can limit this collection through your browser settings, such as blocking or deleting cookies, and through opt-out tools offered by those providers, such as the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

How we use information

We use the information you provide to respond to your inquiry and to manage the appraisal services you request.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information is shared only with the service providers described above as needed to operate this website and respond to you, or where required by law.

Retention

We keep information as long as reasonably needed to respond to your inquiry, provide requested services, and meet legal, regulatory (including appraisal workfile retention), contractual, and business obligations.

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect the information we receive. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your choices and requests

You may contact us using the information below to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, where applicable. Some records may need to be retained for legal, regulatory, contractual, or business purposes.

Changes to this policy
