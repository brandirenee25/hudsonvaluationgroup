Appraising Walnut Creek's Established Market

Walnut Creek is one of Contra Costa County's most active real estate markets — and one where careful, market-supported analysis is especially important. The city encompasses everything from condominium complexes near BART to custom hillside estates in the Northgate and Tice Valley neighborhoods, and pricing these properties requires genuine local market knowledge.

The market's diversity is its defining characteristic. A one-bedroom condo in downtown Walnut Creek and a four-bedroom home on the hillside above Tice Valley require completely different comparable selection and adjustment methodology. Brandi Hudson (#AR029078) has 25 years of residential appraisal experience throughout Contra Costa County and understands these distinctions.

Whether it is a condo near BART or a hillside home in Northgate, each home appraisal is built on recent local sales.

Appraisal Services in Walnut Creek

  • Estate & Trust Appraisals — Walnut Creek has a substantial population of long-term homeowners, many of whom purchased decades ago when values were significantly lower. Estate appraisals here often involve properties with substantial appreciation, where careful documentation matters for stepped-up basis, IRS reporting, and estate or trust administration.
  • Divorce Appraisals — We provide neutral, well-supported valuations for settlement and mediation in Walnut Creek divorce proceedings. Not litigation — focused on helping both parties understand value clearly.
  • Date of Death Appraisals — Often used to help document fair market value for basis purposes on Walnut Creek properties that have appreciated significantly; a CPA or tax attorney should confirm the applicable tax treatment.
  • Pre-Listing Appraisals — With Walnut Creek's varied neighborhoods and price points, an independent appraisal helps sellers price confidently in a competitive market.
  • PMI Removal — Walnut Creek's appreciation trends mean some homeowners may want to ask their servicer about PMI removal.

What Makes Walnut Creek Properties Unique to Appraise

  • BART proximity premiums — Properties near the Walnut Creek BART station can command premiums that require careful market support.
  • Downtown condominium market — The downtown condo market has its own dynamics, HOA fee impacts, and comparable selection challenges distinct from single-family homes.
  • Hillside and view properties — Northgate and Tice Valley hillside homes with Mt. Diablo views require view adjustment methodology specific to this market.
  • Lamorinda spillover — The Lafayette and Orinda border areas create interesting comparable selection questions that require local knowledge to navigate.

Frequently Asked Questions — Walnut Creek Appraisals

Do you appraise condominiums in downtown Walnut Creek?

Yes. Condominium appraisals require specific methodology including HOA fee analysis, project-level review, and careful comparable selection within the same or similar complexes. We have experience with Walnut Creek's downtown condominium market.

How do BART proximity premiums affect Walnut Creek appraisals?

BART proximity can add measurable value in Walnut Creek, particularly for buyers who commute to San Francisco. Adjustments are developed from market-supported analysis, including paired-sales analysis when appropriate and supported by available data. We do not apply a standard percentage.

How long does a Walnut Creek appraisal typically take?

Most assignments are completed within 5–10 business days from inspection. Rush timelines are available for time-sensitive estate or divorce matters — contact us to discuss your specific deadline.
