Homeowner Resource

When a Loved One Dies

A Practical Property & Household Guide

Over the years, I’ve worked with homeowners and families who were handling a recent death and suddenly found themselves responsible for property, utilities, paperwork, accounts, and decisions they had never handled before. More than once, someone has asked me during an appraisal, ‘What do I do next?’ Seeing how overwhelming that can be is what led me to create this resource as a practical starting point.

You do not have to handle everything at once. Start with the section that feels most relevant right now.

Secure the Home

Make sure doors and windows are secure, alarms are working, pets are cared for, and the property is monitored if vacant.

Order Certified Death Certificates

Multiple certified copies may be needed by banks, insurers, agencies, and other institutions.

Locate Important Documents

Find the will, trust, estate-planning documents, insurance policies, deeds, account statements, and key contact information.

Keep Essential Services Active

Do not immediately shut off electricity, gas, water, internet, security, landscaping, or other essential household services.

Protect the Mail

Make sure important mail is not lost and determine who has authority to manage or redirect it.
