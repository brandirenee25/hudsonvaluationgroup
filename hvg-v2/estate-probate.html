Estate & Trust Appraisals in Contra Costa County

When a loved one passes, settling their estate or administering a trust often calls for a credible, independent opinion of property value. We prepare private appraisals of inherited homes for estate settlement, trust administration, date-of-death value, stepped-up basis, and heir buyouts — for families, executors, trustees, CPAs, and their advisors.

What is an estate or trust appraisal?

An estate or trust appraisal is an independent opinion of a residential property's fair market value, prepared by a California Certified Residential Appraiser for private estate settlement and trust administration. It is commonly used for date-of-death value and stepped-up basis, IRS Form 706, trust accounting, equitable distribution or buyouts between heirs, and pricing an inherited home for sale.

A note on probate: in formal California probate, the court-appointed probate referee generally handles the official real-property inventory valuation. Our appraisals are private assignments for executors, trustees, heirs, and their advisors, not referee or court services.

Brandi Hudson (CA Certified Residential Appraiser #AR029078) provides estate and trust appraisals throughout Contra Costa, Alameda, and San Joaquin Counties that are developed and reported in accordance with USPAP and formatted for estate attorneys, trustees, and CPAs.

When is an estate appraisal needed?

  • When a trustee needs to value real property held in a trust
  • When beneficiaries or heirs need an independent value for equitable distribution
  • When the estate is subject to federal estate tax reporting or other tax-related valuation requirements
  • When heirs plan to sell an inherited home or buy out a sibling
  • For trust accounting and fiduciary reporting purposes

Who uses estate appraisals?

Estate appraisals serve a wide range of clients going through the settlement process:

  • Executors and administrators who need to appropriately document the value of estate real property
  • Trustees managing real property held in trust
  • Estate attorneys advising families on estates and trusts
  • CPAs and tax professionals preparing estate tax returns
  • Financial advisors helping families understand total estate value
  • Heirs and beneficiaries who need clarity on what they're receiving

How the process works

Most assignments include an interior and exterior property observation. The scope of work is determined by the assignment conditions, intended use, available data, and applicable appraisal requirements. Market research uses comparable sales data from the relevant time period. The final report documents our methodology, the comparable sales selected, and our well-supported opinion of value.

We communicate clearly throughout the process and are available to answer questions from you, your attorney, or your CPA. Our goal is to make a difficult time as straightforward as possible.

What is included in the report?

  • Full interior and exterior property inspection
  • Market value opinion as of the required effective date
  • Comparable sales analysis with documented support
  • Written report developed in accordance with USPAP
  • Formatted for estate attorneys, trustees, and CPAs
