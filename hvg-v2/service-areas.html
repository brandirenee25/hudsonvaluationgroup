Residential Appraiser Serving Contra Costa County

Hudson Valuation Group provides residential appraisal services throughout Contra Costa County, from the Tri-Valley and Lamorinda to Central County and East County communities along the Delta.

Contra Costa County assignments include waterfront and canal-front homes in the Delta communities as well as established neighborhoods and newer subdivisions. Comparable sales are selected from the most similar available market area for each property.
