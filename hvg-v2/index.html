FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Do you provide date-of-death appraisals?

Yes. Hudson Valuation Group provides retrospective date-of-death appraisals for estate administration, stepped-up cost basis documentation, and other appropriate purposes. A CPA or attorney should determine applicable tax treatment and filing requirements.

Which counties do you serve?

Hudson Valuation Group serves Contra Costa County, Alameda County, and San Joaquin County.

Do you provide litigation support or expert testimony?

No. Hudson Valuation Group does not provide litigation support, expert testimony, depositions, or court appearances.

Do you provide lender-ordered appraisals?

Yes. Hudson Valuation Group works with lenders, mortgage professionals, private lenders, credit unions, and other appropriate residential appraisal clients.

Can an appraisal support PMI removal?

It can. Some lenders may require or accept an appraisal when evaluating a PMI-removal request. The lender determines eligibility, valuation requirements, and the final decision.
