Appraisal to Remove PMI (Private Mortgage Insurance)

Yes, in many cases an appraisal to remove PMI can support your request to drop mortgage insurance. If your home's value has risen since you bought it, a new appraisal may show a lower loan-to-value ratio. Your mortgage servicer sets the eligibility rules and makes the final decision, so call them first to confirm whether they accept a borrower-ordered appraisal.

What is PMI, and when can you remove mortgage insurance?

If your home has appreciated since you purchased it, you may be able to request PMI removal. Contact your mortgage servicer before ordering an appraisal to confirm eligibility and valuation requirements. Some servicers require that they order the appraisal or other valuation directly.

Private mortgage insurance (PMI) is added to your monthly mortgage payment when you originally put less than 20% down. It protects your lender — not you — and adds to your monthly payment. Once you've built sufficient equity through appreciation or payments, you can request its removal.

Is PMI removal based on appraised value?

Servicers typically evaluate a PMI removal request using your loan-to-value (LTV) ratio along with other requirements such as payment history and loan seasoning; the thresholds and rules vary by loan and servicer. If home values in your area have risen since you purchased, a current appraisal may help document your home's value as part of a removal request. Hudson Valuation Group does not determine eligibility; your servicer makes that decision. Hudson Valuation Group does not guarantee PMI cancellation.

Hudson Valuation Group provides PMI removal appraisals, developed and reported in accordance with USPAP, for use where the mortgage servicer permits a borrower-ordered appraisal, throughout Contra Costa, Alameda, and San Joaquin Counties.

How to remove PMI with an appraisal, step by step

  1. Contact your mortgage servicer to confirm eligibility, their PMI removal process, and their valuation requirements
  2. Confirm whether they accept a borrower-ordered appraisal and in what format; some servicers order the appraisal or other valuation directly
  3. If a borrower-ordered appraisal is accepted, order the appraisal from Hudson Valuation Group
  4. Submit the appraisal report to your lender with any required forms
  5. Your servicer reviews the request and decides whether PMI can be removed

What affects your home's current value

  • Market appreciation since your purchase date
  • Improvements and renovations you've completed
  • Condition relative to comparable properties
  • Changes in the surrounding neighborhood or market

PMI removal appraisal FAQ

Can I get my house appraised to remove PMI?

Often, yes. Many servicers will consider a current appraisal when you ask to remove PMI because your home's value has gone up. Some servicers order the appraisal themselves, so confirm their process before you order one.

Is PMI removal based on appraised value?

Servicers usually look at your loan-to-value ratio, along with other requirements such as payment history and how long you have had the loan. A current appraisal can document today's value as part of that review. The servicer decides eligibility.

What should I ask my mortgage servicer before ordering an appraisal?

Ask whether you are eligible to request PMI removal, what loan-to-value they require, whether they accept a borrower-ordered appraisal, and what report format they need.

Does an appraisal guarantee my PMI will be removed?

No. The appraisal provides an independent opinion of market value. Your mortgage servicer decides whether PMI is removed.
